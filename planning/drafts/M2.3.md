## Outcome

Ordinary actions serialize while control requests and deadlines remain responsive, including when the caller disappears.

## Milestone

#2

## Dependencies

#15

## Scope

- Use the chosen GLib loop and monotonic admission deadlines including queue time; express waits/actions as short cancellable steps and observe desktop subprocesses asynchronously.
- Route SIGINT, client disconnect and worker timeout to the corresponding unfinished request; cancel queued work before side effects when its deadline expires.
- Make cancellation, reset and stop bypass the ordinary queue and signal the owning execution context. Define cleanup continuation and result preservation for actions with partial effects.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Deterministic task doubles demonstrate serialization, queued expiry, active cancellation, control bypass and no concurrent input-owner mutation under a slow operation.
- [ ] SIGINT/socket loss cancels only the corresponding ordinary request within the provisional bound; lifecycle cleanup continues after caller departure and completed side effects are not described as undone.
- [ ] Checks use worker-enforced deadlines, not just a client timeout. Later M5/M7 issues explicitly own real-adapter timing validation.
