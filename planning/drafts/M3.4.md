## Outcome

Stop and essential failures clean ordinary descendants within a finite deadline even when the worker or client cannot cooperate.

## Milestone

#3

## Dependencies

#20

## Scope

- Order shutdown hooks as cancellation, tracked release, normal application-window close, then service-manager termination of remaining owned processes within the overall shutdown budget.
- Implement a bounded service cleanup hook that records terminal outcome and removes only generation-owned runtime/settings files, preserving earlier failures and durable artifacts.
- Cover startup failure and independent worker/compositor/private-bus death, worker hang, and client disconnect; use the manager fallback when socket control is unusable. Production app/input hooks are wired in M7.1.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Real ordinary child/grandchild processes disappear within the shutdown bound after normal stop, failed start and each essential-process failure, including a blocked worker.
- [ ] Hook-order checks prove release/close are attempted before escalation; no client connection is required for cleanup to finish.
- [ ] Stale hooks cannot touch replacement-generation files/services; artifacts and a truthful final manifest survive worker death before it can record its outcome.
