## Outcome

Cancellation stops further emission and attempts all tracked releases within a measured bound, without queueing behind an active action.

## Milestone

#5

## Dependencies

#29, #16

## Scope

- Connect the single input owner to completion, SIGINT, client loss, worker timeout, focus/target loss, device failure and shutdown cancellation paths.
- Use short steps and timers, stop future sequence work, release tracked keys/buttons/modifiers and preserve partial/uncertain effects and handles in replies/logs.
- Extend the harness for cancellation during holds and shifted typing with slow asynchronous queries and controlled external work using the M1 capture harness or a delay double; collect emission-stop and release-attempt timing evidence. Production capture responsiveness belongs to M7.4. Preserve ordinary-action serialization: test active input and active capture scenarios separately, including cancellation of queued work.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Separate CLI interruption/disconnection and worker-enforced timeout meet the documented bound during real holds and modified text; fixture receipts show no continuing sequence after cancellation detection.
- [ ] Normal completion and every failure path attempt release; an unconfirmable release is reported and blocks further input rather than silently clearing the ledger.
- [ ] Control bypass works under queued requests and slow desktop calls, with no concurrent libei mutation and no automatic retry or claim that earlier side effects were undone.
