## Outcome

Production adapters are connected to readiness, shutdown and every CLI command; a real-desktop integration suite demonstrates the full normal and failure workflows.

## Dependencies

#3, #4, #5, #6

## Scope

- Replace preliminary probes/hooks with production window/input/capture/app integrations and finalize doctor capability reporting and stable error mapping.
- Assemble the fixtures built in M1/M4/M5 into the complete integration harness, adding controlled process failures and delays where needed; do not defer adapter-critical fixtures to this milestone.
- Exercise separate CLI processes through launch, discover, focus, capture, type, hold/release, click, close and stop, with application acknowledgment before pixel assertions.
- Automate the complete REQ-038 matrix plus duplicate/conflicting start, close timeout, explicit kill, queued deadline expiry, failed reset, capture failure and worker-hang fallback.
- Measure and enforce documented timing limits under slow queries/capture and ensure required cleanup remains independent of clients.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All required command families operate noninteractively against the real private KWin service; complete startup readiness uses production adapters and shutdown attempts actual release and graceful close before fallback.
- [ ] Every REQ-038 condition has an identified test and retained diagnostic evidence; missing required platform capabilities fail the support gate rather than silently passing or being skipped.
- [ ] Tests establish stale-generation protection before side effects (including start/stop races), no host endpoint fallback, no PID-reuse signaling, bounded descendant cleanup and durable terminal manifests for independent essential-process deaths.
- [ ] Actual cancellation/focus-polling/deadline measurements meet the published bounds, including slow desktop calls, client disappearance, modified typing and held input. Uncertain effects are exposed without retries.
